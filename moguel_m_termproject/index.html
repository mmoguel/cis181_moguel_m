Every tool and strategy on this website is designed to support your own thinking. You are still responsible for checking accuracy, following instructor policies, protecting your privacy, and submitting work that reflects your own understanding.

A practical starting point

Before using AI for an assignment

  1. Read the assignment instructions carefully.
  2. Check your instructor’s policy on artificial intelligence.
  3. Decide what part of the work you need help understanding.
  4. Use AI for support, not to replace your own thinking.
  5. Verify facts, quotations, citations, and calculations.
  6. Revise the final work so it accurately reflects your voice.

Remember

AI can produce information that sounds convincing but is inaccurate, incomplete, biased, or entirely invented.

Treat AI output as a starting point that must be reviewed, not as a final academic source.
