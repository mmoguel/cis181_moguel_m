Responsible use

Use AI with judgment, honesty, and care

Responsible AI use means understanding that these tools can be helpful while still producing errors, biased results, weak reasoning, or inappropriate recommendations. Students remain responsible for reviewing their work and following course policies.

Four responsibilities

What responsible AI use requires

Before using an AI response in your schoolwork, consider accuracy, privacy, fairness, and academic integrity.

Check accuracy

AI tools can provide incorrect facts, invented citations, outdated information, or explanations that sound convincing but are wrong.

Verify important information with reliable sources before using it.

Protect privacy

Do not enter passwords, student identification numbers, private records, confidential class materials, or sensitive personal information into an AI system.

Recognize bias

AI systems learn from existing data, which may include stereotypes, missing perspectives, or unfair patterns.

Consider whose viewpoints are represented and whose may be missing.

Follow course policies

Different instructors and colleges may have different rules about when AI may be used and whether its use must be disclosed.

Ask your instructor when the expectations are unclear.

Decision process

Before using AI for an assignment

  1. Read the assignment instructions carefully.
  2. Check the syllabus and course AI policy.
  3. Identify which parts of the work must be completed independently.
  4. Decide whether AI support is appropriate for the specific task.
  5. Avoid entering private or confidential information.
  6. Review and verify every important AI-generated claim.
  7. Disclose your AI use when required.

When in doubt, ask

A simple question to your instructor can prevent misunderstandings and academic integrity problems.

Explain how you plan to use the tool, such as brainstorming, proofreading, or creating practice questions.

Think before you submit

Responsible-use comparison

Examples of responsible and risky AI use
Situation Responsible action Risky action
Essay assignment Use AI to brainstorm ideas, then write and revise the essay yourself. Submit an AI-generated essay as your own work.
Research project Use AI to suggest search terms, then locate and verify real sources. Copy citations from AI without checking whether they exist.
Study planning Use AI to draft a schedule and adjust it to your real responsibilities. Follow an unrealistic schedule without reviewing it.
Personal information Use general descriptions and remove identifying details. Enter private student, financial, medical, or account information.
Class discussion Use AI to clarify a concept, then respond using your own understanding. Post an AI response without reading or understanding it.

Quick self-check

Responsible AI checklist

  • I understand my instructor’s rules for AI use.
  • I completed the main thinking and learning myself.
  • I checked important facts using reliable sources.
  • I confirmed that any citations or quotations are real.
  • I protected private and confidential information.
  • I reviewed the response for bias or missing perspectives.
  • I revised the final work so it reflects my own understanding.
  • I disclosed AI assistance when required.

You are responsible for the final submission

An AI tool cannot take responsibility for inaccurate information, policy violations, poor reasoning, or academic misconduct.

Always read, understand, and verify your work before submitting it.
