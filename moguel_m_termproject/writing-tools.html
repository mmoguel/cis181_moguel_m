Writing support

Use AI to strengthen your writing process

AI writing tools can help you generate ideas, organize your thoughts, revise unclear sentences, and catch grammar mistakes. They should support your writing process, not replace your own ideas or complete assignments for you.

Appropriate uses

Where AI can help

The most useful writing support happens before and after you write your own draft.

Brainstorm ideas

Use AI to explore possible topics, examples, or questions when you are unsure how to begin.

Example prompt: Give me five possible essay topics about the impact of social media on college students.

Create an outline

Ask AI to help organize ideas into a logical sequence after you have selected your own topic and main point.

Example prompt: Help me organize these three ideas into a basic essay outline.

Revise for clarity

Use AI to identify sentences that are confusing, repetitive, or too wordy. Review every suggestion before accepting it.

Example prompt: Explain which sentences in this paragraph are unclear and why.

Proofread carefully

AI can flag possible grammar, spelling, and punctuation issues, but it can also misunderstand your meaning.

Example prompt: Identify possible grammar errors in this paragraph without rewriting it.

Keep your voice

A responsible writing workflow

  1. Read the assignment and grading criteria.
  2. Develop your own main idea or argument.
  3. Write your first draft in your own words.
  4. Use AI for targeted feedback on structure or clarity.
  5. Review each suggestion instead of accepting everything.
  6. Revise the final paper so it still sounds like you.
  7. Disclose AI use if your instructor requires it.

Do not submit AI-generated work as your own

Asking AI to write an entire essay, discussion post, or reflection can violate academic integrity policies.

Even when AI-generated writing sounds polished, it may contain weak reasoning, inaccurate claims, or language that does not reflect your actual understanding.

Compare the approach

Better and weaker uses of AI writing support

Examples of responsible and irresponsible AI writing use
Writing task Responsible use Use to avoid
Choosing a topic Ask for possible directions, then choose and develop your own topic. Ask AI to choose the topic and write the full assignment.
Outlining Use AI to organize ideas you already developed. Use an AI outline without checking whether it matches the assignment.
Revision Ask for feedback on clarity, structure, or repetition. Replace your full draft with an AI rewrite.
Proofreading Review suggested grammar corrections one at a time. Assume every AI correction is accurate.

Best practice

Keep a copy of your original draft. This makes it easier to compare changes, protect your voice, and explain how you developed your work.
